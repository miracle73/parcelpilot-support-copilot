# Product note

## Additional problem addressed

Issue Radar handles proactive detection: P1 incidents, response-target breaches, and current-known-issue matches at the workbook snapshot.

## Next priorities

1. Real SSO/RBAC and audit-grade identity.
2. Ticketing integration with approvals and idempotency.
3. Permission, citation, precedence, and outcome evaluations.
4. Semantic retrieval plus constrained LLM planning.
5. Longer-history baselines for true spike detection.

## Intentionally omitted

Production identity, hosted deployment, external ticket mutation, vector infrastructure, and automated credit payment require systems or decisions absent from the pack.

## Success metric

Primary: percentage of cases resolved correctly without human rework, with zero cross-account disclosures. Supporting metrics: escalation precision and median time-to-first-useful-answer.
